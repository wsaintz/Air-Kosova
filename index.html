About Kosova Air

Fly Kosova, Fly Premium

Welcome to Kosova Air, the national virtual flagship connecting Pristina to high-yield global destinations across Europe, Asia, and North America.

Founded with a focus on premium long-haul efficiency, Kosova Air operates top-tier wide-body aircraft to deliver safe, reliable, and high-capacity service across major international corridors.

Flagship Fleet

Our long-range flagship operations are anchored by the iconic Boeing 747-400 (Registration: Z6-BOE), styled in our distinctive royal blue and gold speed-sweep livery.

Whether bridging key intercontinental hubs like New York (JFK), London Heathrow (LHR), and Tokyo Haneda (HND), Kosova Air maintains a standard of excellence on every route.
